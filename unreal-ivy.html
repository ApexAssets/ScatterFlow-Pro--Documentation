ScatterFlow documentation / Unreal plugin

Unreal: Ivy

Grow and shape climbing plants directly on level geometry

Beta The ScatterFlow plugin for Unreal Engine is in early development. Features, settings, and interface elements may change between releases.

Ivy

Grow climbing ivy over walls, rocks, trees, or the ground. Adjust the growth, leaves, and flowers from the panel, and use blockers to keep areas clear.

Grow ivy

Point the viewport at a surface and click the ivy icon. The ivy grows from that spot.

Ivy can only climb meshes that have collision. Move the ivy actor to change where it starts, and rotate it to change its direction.

Add Ivy and Remove Ivy

The Add Ivy button adds another plant where the viewport is looking.

The Remove Ivy button deletes the selected ivy. Press Ctrl+Z to undo it.

Presets and New Seed

The Light, Overgrown, and Hanging preset buttons set the growth, leaves, and flowers in one step. The plant keeps its position, seed, and meshes.

The New Seed button gives a different arrangement with the same settings.

Growth

Growth sets how much of the plant has grown, from the root outward.
Vine Length sets how far each main vine reaches.
Main Vines is the number of vines that start at the root.
Branching is the number of side branches per metre, which sets how densely the ivy covers the surface.
Climb sets the direction. 1 grows upward, 0 spreads in all directions, and negative values grow downward.
Wander varies the vine paths, from straight at 0 to tangled at 1.
Vine Depth sets how far the vines sit above the surface, so later growth can lie over earlier vines.

Leaves

Density is the number of leaves per metre of vine.
Scale sets the leaf size.
Tilt sets how far the leaf tips lift off the surface.
Leaf Depth layers the leaves away from the surface for a fuller look.

Your own leaf mesh

Use the Leaf Mesh slot to replace the built-in leaf with your own mesh. Put its stem at the origin, with the leaf pointing along +X and facing +Z.

You can mix up to four meshes, and a new slot appears each time you fill one. The weight beside each slot sets how much of the mix it makes up. The arrow and folder buttons work as they do in a scatter layer's Meshes section.

Check Scale after assigning your own mesh. At 1, the mesh keeps its original size, so a large mesh may need a much lower value.

Flowers

Turn on Flowers to add flowers along the vines and show their settings.

The Flower Mesh slot takes your own flower mesh, centred at the origin and facing +Z. It works like Leaf Mesh.
Density is the number of flowers per metre where the vine flowers.
Scale and Scale Variation set the flower size and how much it varies.
Coverage sets how much of the vine has flowers.
Tilt Random varies how far the flowers lean away from the surface.
Offset sets how far the flowers sit above the surface.

Wind

Wind Strength sets how much the leaves and flowers move in the level's wind. At 0, they stay still.
Wind Speed sets how fast they move, compared with the level's wind.

Keeping ivy off windows and signs

The Add Blocker button places a box where the viewport is looking. Resize it over a window, doorway, or anything else you want kept clear, and the nearby ivy updates to match.

Select a blocker to show the Selected Blockers section, where buttons choose how it works:
Grow Around makes the vines grow around the blocker.
Cut Out grows the ivy as normal, then removes everything inside the box for a clean opening.
No Leaves lets vines pass through, but removes leaves and flowers inside.
Box or Round uses the whole box or an oval inside it. Round suits rounded windows and arches.
Padding adds extra space around the blocker.

The Block Selected button makes fitted blockers for other actors. Select those actors together with the ivy, then click it. The blockers follow their actors when you move them.

More Settings

Expand the controls at the bottom of the Ivy section for finer control.

Growth: Branch Depth sets how many times branches split again, Gravity pulls growth downward, Spacing keeps vines apart, and Hang Length limits how far ivy can hang without support.

Vines: Thickness sets the thickness at the root, and Tip Thickness at the ends.

Leaves: turn off Leaves for bare vines. You can also vary the leaves with Scale Variation, Young Leaf Scale (smaller leaves at the tips), Rotation Random, Droop, Tilt Random, Spread, and Colour Variation.

The Add Wind To My Leaf Materials button adds ScatterFlow wind to the materials of your own leaf and flower meshes.

The Use Blockers checkbox sets whether this ivy responds to Ivy Blockers.

Recorded with ScatterFlow Unreal Sync 2.20 (Sky and Weather: 2.23) in Unreal Engine 5.8, linked to ScatterFlow 1.0.9 in Blender 5.2.2.
