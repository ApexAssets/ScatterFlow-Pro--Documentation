ScatterFlow documentation / Unreal plugin

Unreal: Ivy

Grow and shape climbing plants directly on level geometry

Beta The ScatterFlow plugin for Unreal Engine is in early development. Features, settings, and interface elements may change between releases.

Ivy

Generate climbing ivy over walls, rocks, trees, or ground surfaces. Adjust growth, leaves, and flowers from the panel, and use blockers to keep selected areas clear.

Grow ivy

Point the viewport at a surface and click the ivy icon. An ivy actor is created at the targeted location, grows across the surface, and is selected for editing.

Target meshes need collision for the ivy to climb them. Move the ivy actor to change its starting location and rotate it to change its direction.

Add Ivy and Remove Ivy

Add Ivy: creates another plant where the viewport is looking. Reposition the view before clicking to choose a new location.

Remove Ivy: deletes the selected ivy. Use Ctrl+Z to undo the deletion.

Presets and New Seed

Light, Overgrown, and Hanging configure growth, leaves, and flowers. Presets preserve the plant's position, seed, and mesh choices.

New Seed: generates a different arrangement with the current settings.

Growth

Growth: controls how much of the plant is visible from its root outward. Adjust it to reveal the growth progressively.
Vine Length: the reach of each main vine.
Main Vines: the number of vines starting at the root.
Branching: side branches per metre, controlling overall coverage density.
Climb: growth direction. At 1, growth is directed upward; at 0, it spreads in all directions; negative values direct it downward.
Wander: variation in the vine paths, from straight at 0 to tangled at 1.
Vine Depth: the distance vines sit above the surface, allowing later growth to overlap earlier vines.

Leaves

Density: leaves per metre of vine.
Scale: leaf size.
Tilt: how far leaf tips lift away from the surface.
Leaf Depth: layered spacing away from the surface for a fuller appearance.

Your own leaf mesh

Use Leaf Mesh to replace the generated leaf with a custom mesh. Its stem should be at the origin, with the leaf pointing along +X and facing +Z.

Assigning a mesh reveals the next slot, up to four meshes. The weight beside each slot controls its contribution to the mix. The arrow and folder buttons work as they do in a scatter layer's Meshes section.

Adjust Scale after assigning a custom mesh. A value of 1 uses its original size, so larger source meshes may need a substantial reduction.

Flowers

Enable Flowers to display its controls and add flowers along the vines.

Flower Mesh: a custom flower mesh, centred at the origin and facing +Z. Assignment works like Leaf Mesh.
Density: flowers per metre in flowering regions.
Scale and Scale Variation: overall size and variation between flowers.
Coverage: the proportion of the vine that produces flowers.
Tilt Random: random variation in how far flowers lean away from the surface.
Offset: the distance flowers sit above the surface.

Wind

Wind Strength: the response of leaves and flowers to the level's wind. At 0, they remain still.
Wind Speed: movement speed relative to the level's wind.

Keeping ivy off windows and signs

Add Blocker places an exclusion box where the viewport is looking. Resize it over a window, doorway, or other area to keep clear. Nearby ivy regenerates to respond to the blocker.

Select a blocker to display Selected Blockers and choose its behaviour:
Grow Around: vines turn around the blocker.
Cut Out: growth is calculated normally, then everything inside the blocker is removed for a clean opening.
No Leaves: vines can pass through, but leaves and flowers are removed inside the region.
Box or Round: uses the full box or an oval region within it, suitable for rounded windows and arches.
Padding: adds clearance around the blocker.

Block Selected: creates fitted blockers for selected actors. Select the actors together with the ivy, then click the button. The blockers follow their actors when moved.

More Settings

Expand the controls at the bottom of the Ivy section for additional detail.

Growth: Branch Depth controls repeated branching, Gravity pulls growth downward, Spacing separates vines, and Hang Length limits unsupported hanging growth.

Vines: Thickness sets the root thickness, while Tip Thickness sets the size at the ends.

Leaves: disable Leaves for bare vines. Additional controls include Scale Variation, Young Leaf Scale for smaller tip leaves, Rotation Random, Droop, Tilt Random, Spread, and Colour Variation.

Add Wind To My Leaf Materials: adds ScatterFlow wind to the materials of custom leaf and flower meshes.

Use Blockers: determines whether this ivy responds to Ivy Blockers.
