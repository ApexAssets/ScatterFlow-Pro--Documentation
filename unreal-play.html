ScatterFlow documentation / Unreal plugin

Unreal: Play

Explore your level with the supplied ScatterFlow character

Beta The ScatterFlow plugin for Unreal Engine is in early development. Features, settings, and interface elements may change between releases.

Play: walk through your level

The Play button

Position the viewport at the intended starting location and click Play at the top of the panel. The ScatterFlow character starts on the ground beneath the viewport camera. No separate game mode or character setup is required.

The on-screen card lists the controls: W A S D to move, mouse to look, hold Shift to sprint, Space to jump, C to crouch, and V to switch between first- and third-person views. Hold the right mouse button to zoom, use Shift+F1 to release the mouse, and press H to hide the card.

Click Stop in the panel to end the preview.

Play Settings

Character: selects a custom character Blueprint. Leave it at None to use the supplied ScatterFlow character.
Start at Editor Camera: starts beneath the viewport camera. Disable it to use the level's Player Start.
Apply These Settings: applies the camera and movement values below. Disable it to keep the character's own settings.
Show Controls While Playing: toggles the shortcut card.

Third-Person Camera: adjust Distance, Side Offset, and Height Offset to position the camera. Field of View controls the normal view, and Zoom FOV applies while holding the right mouse button. Camera Lag and its speed control how the camera follows movement. First-Person Eye Height sets the height used after switching views with V.

Movement: configure Walk Speed, Crouch Speed, Jump Strength, Gravity Scale, and Air Control, which determines steering while airborne.

Crouch Animation and Sprint: choose the animations and stride length per loop to reduce foot sliding. ScatterFlow supplies these animations, or you can assign the character's own versions.

Changing the camera

Adjust the camera before starting Play. Increase Distance to follow further behind the character, and raise Height Offset for a higher viewpoint.

Recorded with ScatterFlow Unreal Sync 2.20 (Sky and Weather: 2.23) in Unreal Engine 5.8, linked to ScatterFlow 1.0.9 in Blender 5.2.2.
