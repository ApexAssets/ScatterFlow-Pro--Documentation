ScatterFlow documentation / Unreal plugin

Unreal: Play

Explore your level with the supplied ScatterFlow character

Beta The ScatterFlow plugin for Unreal Engine is in early development. Features, settings, and interface elements may change between releases.

Play: walk through your level

The Play button

Move the viewport to where you want to start, then click the Play button at the top of the panel. The ScatterFlow character starts on the ground below the viewport camera. You do not need to set up a game mode or a character.

Use W A S D to move and the mouse to look. Hold Shift to sprint, press Space to jump and C to crouch, and press V to switch between first- and third-person views. Hold the right mouse button to zoom, and press Shift+F1 to release the mouse.

An on-screen card lists these controls; press H to hide it. Click Stop in the panel to end the preview.

Play Settings

Pick your own character Blueprint in the Character field. Leave it at None to use the ScatterFlow character.

The Start at Editor Camera checkbox starts you below the viewport camera. Turn it off to start at the level's Player Start.

Turn on Apply These Settings to use the camera and movement values below. Turn it off to keep your character's own settings. The Show Controls While Playing checkbox shows or hides the controls card.

Third-Person Camera: Distance, Side Offset, and Height Offset place the camera. Field of View sets the normal view, and Zoom FOV is used while you hold the right mouse button. Camera Lag and its speed set how closely the camera follows you. First-Person Eye Height sets the eye height after you switch views with V.

Movement: set Walk Speed, Crouch Speed, Jump Strength, and Gravity Scale. Air Control sets how well you can steer in the air.

Crouch Animation and Sprint: choose the animations, and set the stride length per loop so the feet do not slide. ScatterFlow supplies these animations, or you can use your character's own.

Changing the camera

Set up the camera before you start Play. Raise Distance to follow further behind the character, and Height Offset for a higher view.

Recorded with ScatterFlow Unreal Sync 2.20 (Sky and Weather: 2.23) in Unreal Engine 5.8, linked to ScatterFlow 1.0.9 in Blender 5.2.2.
