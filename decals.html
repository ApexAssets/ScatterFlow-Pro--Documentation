ScatterFlow documentation / Decals tab

Decals

Place logos, markings, stains, and other images on surfaces without changing their materials

Adding a decal PBR maps Decal Objects list Surface Snap Decal Properties

The Decals tab adds an image as a separate object on a surface. Use decals for road markings, signs, cracks, dirt, or other localized details.

Each decal remains editable after placement. You can move it, resize it, change its appearance, or hide it without modifying the underlying surface material.

Adding a decal

Choose an image, then place it

1. Click + beside Decal Objects to open the file browser.

2. Select an image and click Add New Decal. Supported formats are PNG, JPG, TIFF, EXR, TGA, and WebP. Images with transparent backgrounds provide clean outlines around the visible artwork.

3. Move the cursor over the target surface. The preview arrow shows the placement position and orientation. Left-click to confirm the position.

4. Move the mouse vertically to set the size, then left-click.

5. Move the mouse horizontally to set the rotation, then left-click to finish.

The lower-left corner of the viewport shows the current step. Press Esc or right-click to cancel.

Decals preserve the image's proportions. A wide image produces a wide decal. The decal faces outward from the surface where it is placed, including walls, floors, and rocks.

PBR maps

A decal with normal and roughness maps

Select multiple texture maps for the same material with Ctrl-click in the file browser. ScatterFlow connects them to the decal automatically, allowing effects such as a rust stain with colour, normal, and roughness detail.

Map types are identified by their filename endings:

_color, _albedo, _diffuse, or _basecolor: colour.
_n, _nrm, or _normal: normal.
_rough or _roughness: roughness.
_metal or _metallic: metallic.
_alpha, _opacity, or _mask: transparency.
_emit or _emissive: emission.

Map not recognized? Rename it using one of the supported endings above.

Decal Objects list

Select and hide

The list contains every decal in the scene, named after its image. Select a row to display that decal's settings in Decal Properties.

Arrow: selects the decal in the viewport so you can move it with G.

Eye: toggles visibility in both the viewport and renders.

Surface Snap

Slide decals over surfaces

Click the magnet button below + to enable Surface Snap. While active, moving a decal with G keeps it attached to the surface under the cursor and aligns it to face outward.

Use it to move a decal between differently oriented surfaces, such as from the ground onto a wall, without manually adjusting its rotation.

Surface Snap also affects other objects. It enables Blender's face snapping. Click the magnet again when finished to avoid snapping other objects unintentionally.

Decal Properties

Select a decal and expand Decal Properties. Its target surface is shown at the top.

Surface settings

Opacity: how visible the decal is over the surface. Lower it for faded or worn markings.

Emission: adds glow for details such as illuminated signs or screens.

Metallic and Roughness: adjust the surface appearance. Low Roughness produces sharper reflections suitable for stickers or wet paint.

Alpha: the transparency supplied by the image.

Color Adjustment, Damage, Dirt and Blur

Color Adjustment: Hue shifts colours, with 0.5 leaving them unchanged. Saturation controls colour intensity, and Value controls brightness. Use these to recolor a decal without editing its source image.

Damage: removes areas of the decal for a chipped or peeling appearance. Damage Amount is sensitive: values from 0.01 to 0.04 range from small chips to substantial removal. Scale controls chip size, while Detail, Roughness, and Lacunarity shape their edges. Damage Position shifts the pattern.

Dirt: adds a grime tint. Dirt Amount and Dirt Color set its strength and colour, while Wetness and Bump control gloss and apparent surface depth. The effect can be subtle on small decals.

Blur: softens the image. At 1, it becomes a diffuse patch, useful for faint stains or shadow-like details.
