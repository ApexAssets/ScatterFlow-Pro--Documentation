ScatterFlow documentation / Unreal plugin

Unreal: Wind

Set wind for the level and adjust how individual layers respond

Beta The ScatterFlow plugin for Unreal Engine is in early development. Features, settings, and interface elements may change between releases.

Wind

Wind for the whole level

Deselect any layer and expand Wind to configure the level-wide effect. This wind drives ScatterFlow plants, rain, snow, water waves, and ivy.

Enabled: toggles wind.
Strength: controls how far plants bend.
Speed: controls how quickly they sway.
Direction (deg): sets the direction the wind blows toward.
Gustiness: varies the wind in gusts rather than maintaining a steady effect.
Leaf Flutter: adds faster, smaller leaf movement over the main sway.

Realtime viewport required. Enable Realtime in the viewport to preview wind animation. It is enabled by default.

Wind for one layer

Select a layer and expand its Wind section. These controls multiply the level-wide wind values for that layer.

Layer Wind Strength: 0 disables motion for the layer, useful for rocks or rigid assets. A value of 2 doubles its response.

Layer Wind Speed: adjusts the layer's motion speed relative to the level's wind.

Recorded with ScatterFlow Unreal Sync 2.20 (Sky and Weather: 2.23) in Unreal Engine 5.8, linked to ScatterFlow 1.0.9 in Blender 5.2.2.
