ScatterFlow documentation / Godot plugin

Godot: Play

Explore your scene with the supplied ScatterFlow character

Beta ScatterFlow Link for Godot is in early development. Features, settings, and interface elements may change between releases.

Play: walk through your scene

Play from Editor Camera

Position the 3D view at the intended starting location. Expand Play and click ▶ Play from Editor Camera. ScatterFlow saves the scene and opens a game window with the character beneath the editor camera. No separate player or input setup is required.

The on-screen card lists the controls: W A S D to move, mouse to look, hold Shift to sprint, Space to jump, C to crouch, and V to switch between first- and third-person views. Hold the right mouse button to zoom, use the mouse wheel for camera distance, press Esc to release the mouse, and H to hide the card.

Press F8 or use Godot's Stop button to end the preview.

Camera settings

Expand Camera under Play to configure the viewpoint before starting.

First Person / Third Person: the initial view mode.
Third Person Distance: how far the camera follows behind the character.
Side Offset: shifts the camera sideways for an over-the-shoulder view.
Pivot Height Offset: raises or lowers the point the camera follows.
Camera Switch Smoothness: controls the transition when switching views with V.
Third Person Camera Collision: moves the camera in front of obstructions instead of allowing it to pass through walls.
Field of View and Zoom Field of View: the normal view angle and the angle used while holding the right mouse button.
Mouse Sensitivity: the response to mouse movement.

Movement and Character settings

Movement: configure Walk Speed, Sprint Speed, and Crouch Speed in metres per second, along with Jump Speed and Gravity. Ground Response and Air Response control movement responsiveness. Under Body, use Player Radius, Standing Height, Standing Eye, Crouch Height, Crouch Eye, and Crouch Smoothness to configure the character's dimensions and crouching behaviour.

Character: Character Scale adjusts model size, and Facing Offset corrects its orientation. Animation Speed and Turn Smoothness control animation playback and turning.

Use repeated previews to judge movement speed and character scale against the environment after making adjustments.

Recorded with ScatterFlow Link 1.8.0 in Godot 4.7.2, linked to ScatterFlow 1.0.9 in Blender 5.2.2.
