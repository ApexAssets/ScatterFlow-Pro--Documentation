ScatterFlow documentation / Godot plugin

Godot: Play

Explore your scene with the supplied ScatterFlow character

Beta ScatterFlow Link for Godot is in early development. Features, settings, and interface elements may change between releases.

Play: walk through your scene

Play from Editor Camera

Move the 3D view to where you want to start, then click ▶ Play from Editor Camera in the Play section. ScatterFlow saves the scene and starts the game with the character under the editor camera. You do not need to set up a player or controls.

Use W A S D to move, the mouse to look, Shift to sprint, Space to jump, C to crouch, and V to switch between first and third person. Hold the right mouse button to zoom, and use the mouse wheel for camera distance. Esc releases the mouse, and H hides the on-screen controls.

Press F8 or Godot's Stop button to stop.

Camera settings

Set up the view in the Camera section, under Play, before you start.

First Person / Third Person: click one to pick the view you start in.
Third Person Distance: how far behind the character the camera follows.
Side Offset: moves the camera sideways for an over-the-shoulder view.
Pivot Height Offset: raises or lowers the point the camera follows.
Camera Switch Smoothness: how smoothly the view changes when you press V.
Third Person Camera Collision: when on, keeps the camera in front of walls and obstacles instead of passing through them.
Field of View and Zoom Field of View: the normal view angle, and the one used while you zoom.
Mouse Sensitivity: how strongly the view responds to the mouse.

Movement and Character settings

The Movement section sets how the character moves: Walk Speed, Sprint Speed, and Crouch Speed in metres per second, plus Jump Speed and Gravity. Ground Response and Air Response set how responsive movement feels.

Under Body, Player Radius, Standing Height, Standing Eye, Crouch Height, Crouch Eye, and Crouch Smoothness set the character's size and how it crouches.

In the Character section, Character Scale sets the model's size, and Facing Offset fixes which way it faces. Animation Speed and Turn Smoothness control its animation and turning.

Play again after each change to check speed and size against your scene.

Recorded with ScatterFlow Link 1.8.0 in Godot 4.7.2, linked to ScatterFlow 1.0.9 in Blender 5.2.2.
