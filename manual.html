Manual Mode

Place and edit instances directly with nine brushes

Starting Scatter Manager Controls Placing brushes Editing brushes Groups

Starting Manual Mode

Manual Mode lets you place instances by hand, such as a hero tree, plants along a path, or the edge of a clearing. Set the layer's Scattering Type to Manual to open the Manual Mode section. See Scattering Type for how it compares with Procedural mode.

Start Placing and the mode dials

Click Start Placing to show the brush dials in the viewport. Pick a brush by clicking its dial or pressing its number, 1–9. Nothing is placed until you pick one.

Click the active dial again to get back to the full brush list.

Press Esc, Enter, or right-click to leave Manual Mode. Your placements stay on the layer.

Scatter Manager

Choose which objects the placement brushes can use.

Scatter Manager

The list shows the objects from the layer's Instances section. Untick an object to leave it out of new placements, for example to paint only the trees from a mixed collection.

The All button ticks every object; the None button unticks them all.

Enable at least one object. Start Placing cannot begin when every object is disabled.

Controls shared by every brush

Erase and brush size

Hold Ctrl and drag to erase instances. The exceptions are Scale, where Ctrl shrinks instances, and Attract, where it pushes them away.

Hold F and move the mouse sideways to change the brush size. This is the same as the Radius dial.

You can still move around the viewport while painting.

Using the setting dials

Drag a dial sideways to change its value. Hold Shift for finer control, or scroll the mouse wheel over the dial.

Ctrl+click or double-click resets a dial. Click an on/off dial to toggle it.

Every brush has a Radius dial. It starts at 2.0, or 0.7 for Precision.

Placing brushes

1  Area

  • Density 1
  • Delay 1.00
  • Surface Rotation on

Places random instances inside the brush as you drag.

Density is how many instances each step of the stroke adds.

Delay is the distance between steps, not a time. Low values give a continuous band; high values give separate clusters.

Surface Rotation tilts new instances to match the slope. Turn it off to keep them upright.

2  Precision

  • Scale 1.00
  • Mesh Preview on

Places one instance at a time. Press and drag to position it, then release to place it.

Scale sets the size of the next instance.

Click the Swap Preview dial, or press F8, to switch to another object. It works when the layer uses a collection with more than one object ticked in Scatter Manager.

Mesh Preview shows a see-through preview of the object at the cursor. Turn it off to show a simple ring instead.

3  Align

  • Density 1
  • Delay 1.00
  • Surface Rotation on

Works like Area, but turns each instance to face along the stroke. Use it for fences, logs, hedges, or crop rows.

Density, Delay, and Surface Rotation work as in Area, but Align keeps its own values.

Editing brushes

These brushes change the instances you have placed by hand on the active layer. Other layers are not affected.

4  Move

  • Adjust Normals off

Drag to move instances across the surface. The brush picks up whatever is inside it when you start dragging, so make Radius big enough to cover the whole group.

Turn on Adjust Normals to tilt moved instances to match the slope at their new spot.

5  Scale

  • Scale Speed 0.05

Hold the mouse button over instances to grow them, or hold Ctrl to shrink them. Scaling keeps going while you hold the button, so use short presses for small changes.

Scale Speed sets how fast they grow or shrink.

6  Rotate

  • Jitter 0.10

Adds random tilt to instances under the brush, building up the longer you stay over them. Use it to break up an arrangement that looks too even.

Jitter sets how much tilt each step adds.

7  Dilute

  • Probability 0.10
  • Min Distance 0.70

Thins out crowded areas by removing instances that are too close together.

Min Distance is the spacing you want. Instances closer than this may be removed.

Probability is the chance that each of those instances is removed on a pass. Low values thin gradually over several strokes; high values remove more at once.

8  Attract

  • Pull Speed 0.03
  • Adjust Normals off

Hold the mouse button to pull instances toward the cursor. The longer you hold, the tighter the cluster, until they can all end up on the same spot.

Hold Ctrl to push them out to the edge of the brush instead, which is handy for opening a clearing. Increase Radius to affect a larger area.

Pull Speed sets how fast they move. Turn on Adjust Normals to keep them aligned to the surface as they move.

9  Comb

  • Rotate Speed 0.015

Leans instances in the direction you stroke. Use it to flatten grass or give trees a wind-blown look.

Rotate Speed sets how strongly each stroke changes the lean.

Manual Mode on a group

Set a group's Type to Manual to paint all its layers together. Group Spread sets how far apart the layers are placed around the brush. See Group Scattering.
