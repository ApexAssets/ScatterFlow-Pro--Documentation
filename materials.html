Materials

Adjust seasons, snow, dryness, and lawn stripes from the ScatterFlow panel

Seasons Snow Dryness Control Lawn Stripes Material List

The Materials section controls compatible materials on the layer selected in the System List. It is located at the bottom of the Scatter tab.

Plants in the ScatterFlow content pack are configured for these controls. For your own assets, the available settings depend on what their materials support.

Seasons

Season Stage

Moves the planting through Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter. The labels below the slider identify the seasons, while the header shows the current season and progress through it.

For example, the meadow transitions from fresh spring green through pale summer straw and autumn orange to winter brown.

Season Amount controls the strength of the seasonal colour adjustment. At 0, plants retain their original colours.

Season Settings

  • Seasons Blend 0.5
  • Color Variation Blend 5
  • Color Variation Seed 0

These controls vary the seasonal effect across the planting so every plant does not change colour together.

Seasons Blend controls differences in seasonal progress between plants. Lower values produce a more uniform change; higher values let some plants appear further through the season than others.

Color Variation Blend controls the size of areas that change together. Color Variation Seed rearranges those areas.

Leaf Randomness adds variation between individual leaves. Season Colors sets the colours used for each season.

Snow

Leaf Snow Amount

  • Default 0

Controls snow coverage on leaves. At 0, no snow is applied; at 1, the leaves are white. A value around 0.3 produces a light dusting.

This setting is in the Seasons panel below Season Colors. Content pack trees also include Trunk Snow Amount for snow on their bark.

Dryness Control

Dry patches

  • Dry Mask off
  • Spread 0.6
  • Scale 0.15
  • Position 0

Creates uneven areas of dry, yellow vegetation. Set Dry Mask OFF/ON to 1 to enable the effect.

Dryness Spread controls how much of the planting is affected. Dryness Scale controls patch size, and Dryness Position moves the pattern across the surface.

Dry/Height Amount and Dryness Height control how far the dry colouration extends up each plant. Dryness Color sets its colour.

Lawn Stripes

Mowing stripes

  • Stripe Scale 0.23
  • Angle 0°
  • Distortion 0

Creates alternating light and dark bands for a mown lawn. Lawn biomes have the effect enabled by default. Use Stripes ON/OFF to toggle it and Stripe Brightness to adjust the contrast.

Stripe Scale controls band width. Angle rotates the pattern, and Position Offset shifts it sideways.

Distortion makes the boundaries less regular, useful for mowing patterns that should not look perfectly straight.

Stripe Spread Min and Max control the sharpness of the transition between light and dark bands.

Material List

Material List and the link button

The list at the top shows the ScatterFlow materials used by the selected layer. Select a material to display its Main, Translucency, and Wetness settings. These control colour adjustments, light passing through leaves, and the appearance of a wet surface.

The link button beside the Materials heading applies the same adjustments to all listed materials. Unlink them to edit a material individually.
