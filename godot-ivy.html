ScatterFlow documentation / Godot plugin

Godot: Ivy

Grow climbing plants directly on scene geometry

Beta ScatterFlow Link for Godot is in early development. Features, settings, and interface elements may change between releases.

Ivy

Generate ivy over walls, rocks, trees, and ground surfaces. Target geometry needs collision for the plant to climb it.

Growing ivy

Point the 3D view at a surface and click the ivy icon at the top of the dock. An SF Ivy node is created at the target location, grows over the surface, and is selected for editing.

Select a mesh before adding ivy to give it collision automatically if it has none.

Move the ivy node to change its starting location. Growth follows the node's up direction, so rotate it to aim the plant.

Regrow and Randomize Seed

Randomize Seed: generates a different arrangement with the same settings. Random Seed displays the value; enter a previous value to reproduce that arrangement.

Regrow: recalculates growth against the current surroundings. Use it after adding or moving nearby geometry, such as placing a crate against the wall that the ivy should climb.

Generated ivy is saved with the scene. It does not regenerate automatically when the scene opens.

Growth: size and spread

Growth: how much of the plant is visible, progressing outward from the root.

Vine Length: the reach of each main vine, in metres.
Main Vines: the number of vines starting at the root.
Branching: side branches per metre, controlling the density of coverage.

Growth: shape

Climb: the upward growth bias. At 1, growth is directed upward; at 0, it spreads in all directions; negative values direct it downward.

Gravity: downward influence on growth, producing a lower, wider plant.

Adhesion: how strongly vines follow the surface. Higher values help them wrap around edges; lower values allow them to lift away and hang.

Wander: variation in vine paths. At 0, vines are straight; higher values produce more winding, tangled growth.

Growth: finer settings

Branch Depth: repeated levels of branching. Branch Length: the length of side branches.

Twigs: short leafy shoots per metre of vine. Twig Length: their length.

Vine Lift: spacing above the surface for later vines, in metres. Use it to build a layered appearance over earlier growth.

Spacing: the separation between vines from different branches, in metres. At 0, they can overlap.

Hang Length: the maximum unsupported distance a vine can extend before stopping.

Step Length: the distance covered by each growth step. Shorter steps follow surface details more closely.

Search Radius: how far from the node the plant searches for an initial surface.

Max Points: a limit on generated growth. When the limit is reached, side branches and twigs are omitted first.

Vines

Vine Thickness: the radius of a main vine at its root, in centimetres. Tip Thickness: its size near the end.

Vine Color: the stem colour.

Vine Sides: the number of sides used to draw vines. A value of 3 is angular and inexpensive; 12 produces a smoother result.

Hide the leaves temporarily to inspect stem shape and detail.

Leaves

Leaves: toggles leaf visibility.

Leaf Density: leaves per metre of vine.
Leaf Scale: overall leaf size. Leaf Scale Variation: size differences between leaves.
Tip Leaf Scale: relative size of leaves near vine tips.
Bare Trunk: the proportion of each vine that remains leafless from the root upward.

Leaves: how they sit

Leaf Rotation Random: how much each leaf is randomly rotated.
Leaf Droop: downward leaf tilt.
Leaf Tilt and Leaf Tilt Random: how far leaf tips lift away from the surface and how much that angle varies.
Leaf Spread: sideways distance from the vine, in metres.
Leaf Offset: distance away from the surface, in metres.
Leaf Shadows: enables leaf shadows. Disable it to reduce rendering cost.

Flowers

Enable Flowers to add them along the vines.

Flower Density: flowers per metre in flowering regions.
Flower Coverage: the proportion of the vine that produces flowers.
Flower Scale and Flower Scale Variation: overall size and variation between flowers.
Flower Tilt Random: variation in the angle away from the surface.
Flower Offset: distance from the surface, in metres.

Wind

Wind Strength: the response of leaves and flowers to global wind. At 0, they remain still.
Wind Speed: motion speed relative to global wind speed.
