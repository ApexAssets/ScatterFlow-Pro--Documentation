ScatterFlow documentation / Godot plugin

Godot: Ivy

Grow climbing plants directly on scene geometry

Beta ScatterFlow Link for Godot is in early development. Features, settings, and interface elements may change between releases.

Ivy

Grow ivy over walls, rocks, trees, and the ground. Ivy only climbs geometry that has collision.

Growing ivy

Aim the 3D view at a surface and click the ivy icon at the top of the dock. This adds an SF Ivy node there, which grows over the surface.

If a mesh has no collision, select it before adding the ivy and it gets collision automatically.

Move the ivy node to change where it starts. The ivy grows along the node's up direction, so rotate the node to aim it.

Regrow and Randomize Seed

Click the Randomize Seed button for a different arrangement with the same settings. The current value is shown in the Random Seed field. Enter an earlier value to get that arrangement back.

The Regrow button grows the ivy again to fit its current surroundings. Click it after you add or move nearby geometry, such as a crate against the wall, that the ivy should climb.

Ivy is saved with the scene. It does not regrow by itself when the scene opens.

Growth: size and spread

Growth sets how much of the plant is shown, from the root outward.

Vine Length sets how far each main vine reaches, in metres. Main Vines sets how many vines start at the root. Branching sets the side branches per metre, which controls how dense the coverage is.

Growth: shape

Climb sets how much the ivy grows upward. At 1, it grows up. At 0, it spreads in all directions. Negative values send it down. Gravity pulls the growth down, for a lower, wider plant.

Adhesion sets how closely vines follow the surface. Higher values help them wrap around edges. Lower values let them lift away and hang.

Wander makes the vine paths more winding. At 0, the vines are straight.

Growth: finer settings

Branch Depth sets how many levels of branching there are, and Branch Length how long the side branches are. Twigs sets the short leafy shoots per metre of vine, and Twig Length their length.

Vine Lift raises later vines above the surface, in metres, for a layered look over earlier growth. Spacing keeps vines from different branches apart, in metres. At 0, they can overlap.

Hang Length is how far a vine can reach without support before it stops. Step Length is the distance of each growth step. Shorter steps follow surface detail more closely.

Search Radius sets how far from the node the ivy looks for a surface to start on. Max Points limits how much ivy is generated. When the limit is reached, side branches and twigs are left out first.

Vines

Vine Thickness sets the radius of a main vine at its root, in centimetres, and Tip Thickness its size near the end. Vine Color sets the stem colour.

Vine Sides sets how many sides the vines are drawn with. Fewer sides are cheaper but look angular.

Hiding the leaves makes the stems easier to check.

Leaves

Leaves turns the leaves on or off. Leaf Density sets the leaves per metre of vine.

Leaf Scale sets the leaf size, and Leaf Scale Variation how much it varies. Tip Leaf Scale sets the size of leaves near the vine tips.

Bare Trunk sets how much of each vine stays bare, starting from the root.

Leaves: how they sit

Leaf Rotation Random rotates each leaf by a random amount, and Leaf Droop tilts the leaves down. Leaf Tilt sets how far leaf tips lift away from the surface, and Leaf Tilt Random how much that varies.

Leaf Spread and Leaf Offset set how far leaves sit from the vine and from the surface, in metres.

Leaf Shadows turns on leaf shadows. Turn it off to save rendering cost.

Flowers

Turn on Flowers to add flowers along the vines. Flower Coverage sets how much of the vine flowers, and Flower Density how many flowers per metre grow there.

Flower Scale and Flower Scale Variation set the flower size and how much it varies. Flower Tilt Random varies their angle from the surface, and Flower Offset sets their distance from it, in metres.

Wind

Wind Strength sets how much the leaves and flowers move in the global wind. At 0, they stay still. Wind Speed sets how fast they move compared with the global wind speed.

Recorded with ScatterFlow Link 1.8.0 in Godot 4.7.2, linked to ScatterFlow 1.0.9 in Blender 5.2.2.
