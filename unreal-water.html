ScatterFlow documentation / Unreal plugin

Unreal: Water

Create water surfaces and adjust their appearance, motion, and character interaction

Beta The ScatterFlow plugin for Unreal Engine is in early development. Features, settings, and interface elements may change between releases.

Water

Add lakes, ponds, oceans, or pools with moving waves, foam, and underwater effects. Start from a preset, then adjust it to suit your scene.

Add water

Point the viewport where you want the water and click the water-drop icon. A flat water body is added there.

Move it to the water level you want, so it cuts into the terrain. Foam and depth colour appear where the ground meets the water, so low parts of the terrain become a lake.

To add more water bodies, click the Add Water button in the Water section.

Water Presets

Click a preset button, such as Lake, Ocean, or Swamp, to set the colour, waves, ripples, caustics, and foam in one step. The water keeps its size and position.

Look

Water Color is the colour of deep water.
Clarity is how far you can see into the water, in centimetres, before its colour takes over.
Turbidity makes the water cloudy, from clear and dark at 0 to milky at 1.
Refraction sets how much the waves distort what is below the surface.

Waves

Wave Height and Wave Length set the height and spacing of the large waves, in centimetres.
Wave Speed sets how fast they move.
Choppiness shapes the crests, from rounded swells at 0 to sharp peaks at 1.
Wave Direction sets which way the waves travel.
Follow Wind makes the waves travel with the level's wind.

Ripples & Caustics

Ripple Strength, Ripple Size, and Ripple Speed control the small movement on the surface.

Caustic Strength, Caustic Size, and Caustic Speed control the moving light patterns under the water.

Foam

Shore Foam and Shore Foam Width add foam in shallow water and around objects that cut through the surface.

Crest Foam adds foam to steep wave crests. If you cannot see it, raise the wave height first.

Underwater

Turn on the Underwater Look checkbox to add coloured haze, light shafts, and caustics when the camera is below the surface.

Underwater Fog sets how thick the haze is. At 1, it matches the Clarity you see from above the surface.
Light Shafts adds visible rays of sunlight through the water.

Character

Turn on the Affects Character checkbox to let your character interact with the water in Play. Wading slows you down, the water carries you, deep water makes you float, and moving leaves ripples on the surface.

Wade Drag sets how much wading slows you down. It reaches full strength at waist depth.
Character Ripples sets the strength of the rings and foam you leave as you move through the water.

Recorded with ScatterFlow Unreal Sync 2.20 (Sky and Weather: 2.23) in Unreal Engine 5.8, linked to ScatterFlow 1.0.9 in Blender 5.2.2.
