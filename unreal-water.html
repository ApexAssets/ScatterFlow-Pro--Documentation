ScatterFlow documentation / Unreal plugin

Unreal: Water

Create water surfaces and adjust their appearance, motion, and character interaction

Beta The ScatterFlow plugin for Unreal Engine is in early development. Features, settings, and interface elements may change between releases.

Water

Add lakes, ponds, oceans, or pools with animated waves, depth-based colour, caustics, foam, and underwater effects. Start with a preset, then adjust it to suit the environment.

Add water

Point the viewport at the target location and click the water-drop icon. A flat water body is created and selected, displaying its settings.

Move it to the intended water level so it intersects the terrain. Shoreline foam and depth colouration appear where the ground meets the water, allowing low areas of the terrain to form a lake.

Use Add Water in the Water section to create additional water bodies.

Water Presets

Lake, Pond, Ocean, Tropical, Pool, and Swamp configure colour, waves, ripples, caustics, and foam together. Applying a preset preserves the water body's size and position.

Look

Water Color: the colour of deep water. Click the swatch to change it.
Clarity: the visibility distance through the water in centimetres, before its colour obscures the view.
Turbidity: the cloudy appearance of the water. At 0, it is clear and dark; at 1, it is milky.
Refraction: how strongly waves distort the view beneath the surface.

Waves

Wave Height and Wave Length: the height and spacing of large waves, in centimetres.
Wave Speed: the speed of wave movement.
Choppiness: the shape of the crests, from rounded swells at 0 to sharper peaks at 1.
Wave Direction: the direction waves travel.
Follow Wind: uses the level's wind direction for wave travel.

Ripples & Caustics

Ripple Strength, Ripple Size, and Ripple Speed: the smaller-scale surface movement.

Caustic Strength, Caustic Size, and Caustic Speed: the moving light patterns beneath the water.

Foam

Shore Foam and Shore Foam Width: foam in shallow areas and around objects intersecting the water.

Crest Foam: foam on steep wave crests. Increase wave height first when evaluating this effect.

Underwater

Underwater Look: enables coloured haze, light shafts, and caustics when the camera is below the surface.

Underwater Fog: haze density. A value of 1 matches the Clarity setting used above the surface.
Light Shafts: sunlight rays visible through the water.

Character

Affects Character: enables water interaction during Play. Wading slows movement, water carries the character, deeper water provides flotation, and movement creates surface ripples.

Wade Drag: the strength of the slowdown, reaching its full effect at waist depth.
Character Ripples: the strength of the rings and foam created by movement through the water.

Use Play to test the transition from walking on land to wading or floating, then back onto the shore.

Recorded with ScatterFlow Unreal Sync 2.20 (Sky and Weather: 2.23) in Unreal Engine 5.8, linked to ScatterFlow 1.0.9 in Blender 5.2.2.
