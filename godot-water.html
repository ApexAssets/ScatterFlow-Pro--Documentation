ScatterFlow documentation / Godot plugin

Godot: Water

Create water surfaces with animated waves, depth-based colour, refraction, and foam

Beta ScatterFlow Link for Godot is in early development. Features, settings, and interface elements may change between releases.

Water

Add ponds, lakes, oceans, pools, or swamps from ready-made presets. The water comes with waves, caustics, and foam where it meets other surfaces.

Shallow water stays clear, and the colour builds with depth. Reflections are strongest when you look across the water at a low angle.

Adding water

Aim the 3D view where you want the water and click the water-drop icon at the top of the dock. This adds an SF Water node there.

Set its height to the water level you want, and set the Size X and Size Y fields, in metres, to cover the area. Any terrain below the water level ends up under water, so a hill can become an island and a hollow a lake.

Resolution sets how many surface points there are per metre. Higher values give smoother large waves but cost more performance.

Use Forward+ or Mobile. ScatterFlow water needs scene depth, so it does not display correctly with the Compatibility renderer.

Presets

The Preset list offers Lake, Pond, Ocean, Tropical, Pool, Swamp, and River.

A preset sets the colour, clarity, waves, and caustics, but keeps the water's size and position. Pick the closest one, then adjust it.

Look

Water Color is the colour of deeper water. Shallow water stays clearer.

Turbidity makes the water cloudier, so the bottom disappears sooner. Clarity scales how far you can see into the water compared with the preset. At 2, you see twice as deep.

Roughness softens the reflections. Lower values make them sharper. Refraction sets how much the waves distort the view below the surface. At 0, there is no distortion.

Caustics sets the moving sunlight pattern under shallow water.

Foam sets how much foam forms along shorelines and around objects in the water. Foam Width sets how wide that band is, in metres.

Whitecaps adds foam to steep wave crests, so it shows most on rough water such as the Ocean preset. See Waves for the wave controls. Sun Through Waves sets the glow of sunlight through wave crests when you look toward the sun.

Waves

Wave Height and Wave Length set how tall the waves are and how far apart, in metres. Short, tall waves give choppy water. Long, low waves give a gentle swell.

Wave Speed and Wave Direction set how fast and which way the waves travel.

Whitecaps are easiest to judge on rough water from a low angle. If large waves look angular, raise Resolution, keeping an eye on performance.

Recorded with ScatterFlow Link 1.10.2 in Godot 4.7.2, linked to ScatterFlow in Blender 5.2.2.
