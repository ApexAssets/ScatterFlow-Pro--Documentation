Content pack and the Asset Browser

Browse ScatterFlow plants, rocks, and props, then place them directly or use them in scatter layers

Adding the library Browsing the pack Dropping assets in Scattering an asset

The content pack has plants, rocks, props, and effects. The biomes use them, but you can also add them one at a time from Blender's Asset Browser.

Adding the library

To see the content pack in the Asset Browser, add its folder to Blender's own asset libraries once. This is separate from ScatterFlow's Asset Libraries, which tell biomes where to find their models.

Set the asset library path

In Edit > Preferences, open Asset Libraries, click the + button, and choose the ScatterFlow_Pro_ContentPack folder. Pick the folder itself, not a file inside it.

Set the Import Method menu to Append, so assets you add become editable copies in your file.

The library then appears in the library menu of every Asset Browser.

Update the path after moving the library. If the assets disappear after you move the folder, set the Path field here to its new location.

Browsing the pack

Library, catalogs and search

Open an Asset Browser, for example by switching the Timeline to one, and choose ScatterFlow_Pro_ContentPack from the library menu.

The catalogs sort the assets into Foliage, Ivy, Trash & Debris, Water, Weather, and Atmosphere. Ivy holds the leaves, flowers, and presets for the Ivy tools.

Click a catalog to filter the assets, or expand it to browse its subcategories. The Search field finds assets by name.

Initial loading may take longer. The library is large, so previews can take a moment to appear the first time.

Dropping assets in

Drag and drop

Drag an asset into the 3D viewport. It lands on the surface under your cursor.

Most assets hold several variations, which arrive in a row inside a collection named after the asset. Move them around or delete the ones you do not need.

Materials are included. Use Material Preview or Rendered shading to see the textures.

For props you place by hand, Randomize Transform adds variety and Snap to Ground places them on the terrain.

Scattering an asset

From an asset to a scatter layer

An asset's collection of variations can be scattered directly.

Drag the asset into the scene, choose an Emitter in the ScatterFlow panel, and click the + button to add a layer. In the Instances section, set the Type menu to Multiple Instances and pick the asset's collection. Then raise Density / m² to fill the surface with its variations.

From there, adjust the layer like any other, with Scale, Rotation, masks, and Wind. Supported content pack materials also respond to the season, snow, and dryness settings.

You can hide the original objects or move them out of view, but keep them in the file. The layer uses them as its source.

Recorded in ScatterFlow 1.0.9 on the ScatterFlow build of Blender 5.2.2, using the ScatterFlow content pack.
